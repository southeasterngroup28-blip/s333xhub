Delete your account

Deleting your account removes your profile, display name, email, profile photo, chat messages, comments, notification preferences and block list. This cannot be undone. Photos and voice notes you sent in chat and files attached to fan mail are removed within 30 days. Unlocked posts are not refunded; refunds are handled by Apple under its store policies. Records of shop orders and show tickets are kept for tax purposes with your account no longer attached to them.

In the app

In the app, open Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. Confirm once and the account is gone immediately.

Without the app

Email support@s333xhub.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my S333XHUB account". We verify it is you and delete the account within 30 days, then confirm by reply.
